# Agent Stylebooks v0.2.0 — Domain & Standards Writing

Agent Stylebooks grows from 11 institutional editorial systems to a 16-stylebook standard
library with dedicated systems for high-stakes and specialist communication.

Existing 11 skills are unchanged.

## Highlights

- **Health communication:** `cdc-clear-communication` organizes verified public-health
  information around one message, an explicit action, meaningful numbers, and contextual risk.
- **Patient information:** `nhs-health-content` keeps clinical boundaries, urgency,
  safety-netting, uncertainty, health literacy, and inclusion visible.
- **Financial disclosure:** `sec-plain-english` clarifies investor communication while mapping
  every material fact and qualification through the rewrite.
- **Technical specifications:** `w3c-technical-reports` separates normative requirements from
  informative explanation and refuses to invent conformance behavior.
- **Engineering reports:** `nasa-technical-writing` preserves measurements and test conditions
  while tracing evidence through results, limitations, and conclusions.

## Catalog and contribution experience

The README now explains the project in the first screen, offers task-based selection, compares
stylebooks with generic prompts, and shows original miniature rewrites. `CATALOG.md` provides
the deeper browse view. The matrix now distinguishes artifact, formality, personality, and
precision. New issue forms collect source authority, differentiation, licensing, and concrete use.

## Provenance and safety

Every addition has an exact source record. CDC and SEC are open/public-domain grounded. NHS,
W3C, and NASA use conservative reference-only treatment. None of the additions claims source
organization endorsement, certification, legal or medical compliance, standards status, or
publication approval.

ASD-STE100 remains a research recommendation, not a production skill. A future adapter should
use a user-supplied official Issue 9 copy at runtime and must distinguish general controlled-
English assistance from a verified check against that copy.
