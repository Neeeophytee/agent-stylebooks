## Preserve constraints

Add these instructions to any invocation when relevant:

```text
Preserve code, URLs, legal meaning, names, numbers, and claims. Mark unsupported
facts as questions instead of filling gaps. Do not claim the organization wrote
or endorsed the result.
```
